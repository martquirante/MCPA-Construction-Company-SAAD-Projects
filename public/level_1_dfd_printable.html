2. Decomposed Sub-Processes (Level 1 Breakdown)

Process 1.0: User Authentication & Account Management

Nangangasiwa sa pagpaparehistro, pag-verify ng email OTP codes, face liveness KYC, at access credentials para sa Client, Staff, at Administrator.

  • Inputs: Client Registration Details, Email Verification Code, Login Credentials (Client, Staff, Administrator), Client Account Management Data.
  • Outputs: Account Registration Status, Verification Status, Authentication Status, Client Account Information.
  • Data Store: D1: User Accounts Store (users, otp_codes).

Process 2.0: Project Inquiry & Site Feasibility Processing

Nagpoproseso ng client inquiries, Philippine geographic location data, GPS theodolite site survey coordinates, at architect feasibility assessments.

  • Inputs: Project Inquiry Details, Project Site Details, Project Requirements and Budget, Inquiry Review and Status Update.
  • Outputs: Inquiry Submission Status, Site Feasibility Report, Updated Inquiry Pipeline Data.
  • Data Store: D2: Inquiries & Site Data Store (client_briefs).

Process 3.0: Consultation & Meeting Scheduling

Namamahala sa pagtatakda ng mga pulong sa pagitan ng kliyente at lead architect (Face-to-Face sa opisina/site o native WebRTC video room consultation).

  • Inputs: Consultation Booking Details (Client), Meeting & Consultation Details (Admin).
  • Outputs: Booking Confirmation and Schedule (Client), Consultation Schedule Confirmation (Admin).
  • Data Store: D3: Consultation Schedules Store (client_briefs, meeting_history).

Process 4.0: Design Blueprint, Costing & Quotation Management

Humahawak sa pagbuo ng itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ), material takeoff calculations, preliminary cost estimates, at CAD blueprint sets.

  • Inputs: Budget & Quotation Data (Staff/Admin), Material & Quantity Takeoff Data (Staff), Blueprint & Technical Plan Updates (Staff).
  • Outputs: Preliminary Cost Estimation (Client), Interactive Design Schematics (Staff), Itemized BOQ Draft (Staff), Cost Estimates & Quotation Records (Staff/Admin).
  • Data Store: D4: Blueprints & Quotations Store (quotations, cloud_blueprints).

Process 5.0: Construction Tracking, Site Progress & Compliance Management

Nagpapatakbo sa Phase 4 site mobilization, photo/video progress logs, weather telemetry, delay tracking, at LGU permit/clearance compliance.

  • Inputs: Project Management Data (Staff), LGU Clearance Submissions (Staff), Site Photos & Progress Logs (Staff), Document & Permit Management Data (Admin), Project & Site Progress Updates (Admin).
  • Outputs: Submission Confirmation & Status (Staff), LGU Compliance Status View (Staff), Project Information & Status (Staff), LGU Compliance Records (Admin), Updated Site Logs & Progress Reports (Admin).
  • Data Store: D5: Construction Tracking & Compliance Store (site_projects, site_milestones, site_photo_logs, delay_events).

Process 6.0: Project Showcase & Public Catalog Management

Nangangasiwa sa pag-publish at pag-curate ng mga natapos na architectural projects sa public showcase/portfolio.

  • Inputs: Project Showcase Management Data (Admin).
  • Outputs: Project Showcase Information (Admin).
  • Data Store: D6: Project Showcase Store (projects).
